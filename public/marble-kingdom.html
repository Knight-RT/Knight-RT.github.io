    How this simulation works

    Armies roam together toward randomly chosen towns or map locations. They engage enemies encountered nearby. The world map shows army markers and town-based territory. The encounter view follows actual fighters, automatically selecting nearby opposing armies; use Follow army to choose one yourself. Weapons animate when damage is dealt. Rivers and terrain are decorative in this version. Kings travel with their armies, marked with crowns; losing a king does not remove its surviving fighters.

    Hold a town uncontested for three seconds to conquer it. Friendly homes and towns produce one reserve fighter per recruitment interval. Reserves join when a friendly army visits, up to its fighter limit. Capturing a town clears its old reserves. Towns become neutral when their kingdom loses its last fighter and king; reserves alone cannot keep a kingdom alive.

    Each kill grants one XP. Set kills per level in the workshop. Level-ups cycle through +25% base fighter health, +10% base speed, and equipment granting +20% base attack (rounded up) and +2 armor. Damage is at least one. The progression table shows the top 12 living fighters. Defeat every enemy king and every remaining enemy fighter to win.

    Settings save locally on this device, not the current battle. Animation starts only when you press Start; pause at any time. This is an independent simulation using the rules described, with the specific capture timing and perk values chosen for this version.
